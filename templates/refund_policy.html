Cancellation & Refund Policy

Effective date: {{ site.EFFECTIVE_DATE }}

1. Before Activation

If you complete payment but the {{ site.PLAN_DURATION_DAYS }}-day plan has not yet been activated (i.e. onboarding has not been completed and Day 1 has not been sent), you may request a full refund by contacting {{ site.SUPPORT_EMAIL }}.

2. After Activation

Once your plan is activated and Day 1 has been delivered, the payment is generally non-refundable, since access to the personalized {{ site.PLAN_DURATION_DAYS }}-day plan and daily check-ins has already begun. Refund requests after activation are reviewed on a case-by-case basis.

3. Non-Refundable Situations

  • Change of mind after the plan has been activated and check-ins have started.
  • Partial use of the {{ site.PLAN_DURATION_DAYS }}-day period.
  • Failure to engage with check-ins once activated.

4. Failed or Duplicate Payments

If a payment fails but funds are deducted, or if you are charged more than once for the same plan, contact {{ site.SUPPORT_EMAIL }} with your payment reference. Verified failed or duplicate payments are refunded in full.

5. Refund Processing Time

Approved refunds are processed back to the original payment method via Razorpay within {{ site.REFUND_PROCESSING_DAYS }} of approval. Actual credit timing may depend on your bank or payment provider.

6. How to Request a Refund

Email {{ site.SUPPORT_EMAIL }} with your registered WhatsApp number and payment reference/date. We'll confirm eligibility and next steps.
